Example itineraries

Sample tours, planned day by day

These are starting points, not fixed schedules. Ask us to adapt the route, pace and duration to your dates.

Safari in Awash & the Rift Valley3 days · Wildlife, waterfall & lake+
  1. Day 1
    Addis Ababa → Awash National Park

    Drive through the Rift Valley, stop in Adama, visit Awash Falls and settle into your lodge.

  2. Day 2
    Awash wildlife & Filwoha hot springs

    Take morning and afternoon game drives to look for oryx, gazelles, baboons and birds; visit Filwoha hot springs.

  3. Day 3
    Lake Ziway → Addis Ababa

    Enjoy lakeside birdwatching and a boat trip before returning to Addis Ababa.

Highland adventure & crater lakes4 days · Wonchi, trails & Lake Langano+
  1. Day 1
    Wonchi Crater Lake

    Hike along the crater rim and take a boat to the island monastery.

  2. Day 2
    Wonchi trails & camping

    Walk the ridges through farmland and forest, then enjoy dinner at camp.

  3. Day 3
    Lake Langano

    Relax beside the lake and, if time allows, visit Abijatta-Shalla National Park.

  4. Day 4
    Return to Addis Ababa

    Take a relaxed morning before the scenic drive back to the capital.

Addis Ababa city & coffee culture2 days · Museums, markets & Entoto+
  1. Day 1
    National Museum, Holy Trinity & Merkato

    Visit the National Museum, Holy Trinity Cathedral and explore Merkato with a local guide.

  2. Day 2
    Entoto Hill & coffee ceremony

    See the city from Entoto, visit its eucalyptus forest and enjoy a traditional coffee ceremony.

Northern historic route5 days · Bahir Dar, Gondar & Lalibela+
  1. Day 1
    Bahir Dar & Lake Tana

    Arrive in Bahir Dar and take a boat trip to Lake Tana's island monasteries.

  2. Day 2
    Blue Nile Falls → Gondar

    Visit the Blue Nile Falls, then travel to Gondar.

  3. Day 3
    Gondar's royal enclosure

    Explore Fasil Ghebbi, Fasilides' Bath and historic churches.

  4. Day 4
    Fly to Lalibela

    Travel to Lalibela and visit its remarkable rock-hewn churches.

  5. Day 5
    Lalibela → Addis Ababa

    See more of the church complex before returning to Addis Ababa.

Omo Valley cultural journey5 days · Jinka, Konso & Turmi+
  1. Day 1
    Arrive in Jinka

    Settle in and visit the South Omo Research Centre or the local market, depending on the day.

  2. Day 2
    Mago National Park

    Visit the park and arrange a respectful, locally guided community visit.

  3. Day 3
    Konso

    Explore Konso's distinctive terraced landscape and fortified villages.

  4. Day 4
    Turmi & Hamer country

    Visit Turmi and learn about Hamer traditions with a local guide.

  5. Day 5
    Return journey

    Travel toward Arba Minch and connect onward, with stops arranged to suit your transport.

Day trips from Addis Ababa3 days · Monastery, Tiya & Wonchi+
  1. Day 1
    Debre Libanos & Portuguese Bridge

    Visit the monastery and enjoy views of the Jamma Gorge.

  2. Day 2
    Tiya & Adadi Mariam

    Explore the UNESCO-listed stelae at Tiya and the rock-hewn church at Adadi Mariam.

  3. Day 3
    Wonchi Crater Lake

    Hike by the crater lake and, conditions permitting, take a boat to its island monastery.

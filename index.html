Example itineraries

Pick a way to travel, see it day by day

Open any style of tour for a sample route, one stop per day. Send us your dates and we rebuild it around the time you have.

Safari Trips3 days · Awash & the Rift Valley+
  1. Day 1Addis Ababa → Awash National ParkDrive south through the Rift Valley with a stop at Adama, then walk down to Awash Falls and settle into your lodge.
  2. Day 2Full day game driveMorning and afternoon drives for beisa oryx, Soemmering's gazelle, baboons and 450+ bird species, plus the hot springs at Filwoha.
  3. Day 3Lake Ziway → Addis AbabaBirdwatching by boat on Lake Ziway among hippos and pelicans, then back to the capital by evening.
Adventure Tours4 days · Crater lakes & highland trails+
  1. Day 1Wonchi Crater LakeHike the rim of the crater and take a boat across the lake to the island monastery, with mule support if you prefer.
  2. Day 2Wonchi trails & campingA longer ridge walk through farmland and forest, dinner and a campfire under the highland sky.
  3. Day 3Lake LanganoSwim and relax on the shores of the Rift Valley's safest swimming lake, with a stop at Abijatta-Shalla National Park.
  4. Day 4Back to Addis AbabaSlow morning at the lake, then the scenic drive home with a coffee stop on the way.
City Tours2 days · Addis Ababa+
  1. Day 1Museums & MerkatoNational Museum (home of Lucy), Holy Trinity Cathedral, then the sprawling stalls of Merkato with a local guide.
  2. Day 2Entoto & coffee culturePanoramic views from Entoto Hill, the eucalyptus forest, a traditional coffee ceremony and an evening of live Ethiopian music.
Historical Tours5 days · The northern historic route+
  1. Day 1Fly to Bahir DarBoat trip on Lake Tana to the island monasteries of Ura Kidane Mehret and Azwa Maryam.
  2. Day 2Blue Nile Falls → GondarWalk to the Tis Abay falls in the morning, then drive to the old imperial capital of Gondar.
  3. Day 3Gondar castlesFasil Ghebbi royal enclosure, Fasilides' bath, Debre Berhan Selassie church and the Empress Mentewab complex.
  4. Day 4Fly to LalibelaAfternoon visit to the northern cluster of rock-hewn churches, including Bete Medhane Alem.
  5. Day 5Lalibela → Addis AbabaSunrise at Bete Giyorgis, the southeastern cluster, then the flight back to Addis Ababa.
Cultural Experiences5 days · Omo Valley & Konso+
  1. Day 1Fly to JinkaSettle in and visit the South Omo Research Centre and the colourful Jinka Saturday market.
  2. Day 2Mago National ParkMeet the Mursi communities of the Mago river lowlands, then return to Jinka for the evening.
  3. Day 3Konso & the KaroThe terraced hillsides and fortified villages of Konso, then the Karo people's painted villages above the Omo river.
  4. Day 4Hamer countryTurmi market and a Hamer village visit, with the chance to see a traditional evangadi dance.
  5. Day 5Return to Addis AbabaDrive to Arba Minch for the flight back, with a stop at the Dorze weaving villages.
Day Trips3 days · Out of Addis and back by dinner+
  1. Day 1Debre Libanos & the Portuguese BridgeA cliffside monastery, the 16th-century bridge over the Jamma gorge and Gelada baboons along the rim.
  2. Day 2Tiya & Adadi MariamThe UNESCO stelae field at Tiya and the rock-hewn church of Adadi Mariam, with a stop at Melka Kunture.
  3. Day 3Wonchi Crater LakeA full day at the crater lake — hike the rim, boat to the island monastery, then drive back through Ambo.
